About Me

Bridging low-level microcontrollers and digital circuits with modern, scalable software engineering.

I enjoy understanding how technology works at every layer—from digital circuits and ultrasonic sensors to real-time software. In 2024, I built an Arduino Mega Ultrasonic Radar System with live sonar sweep visualization, and in 2025 I engineered a Smart RFID Attendance System for the College Hobby Fair that sends real-time Bangla alerts to guardians.
